About

Ahmed Ololade, known professionally as Asake, is a Nigerian Afrobeats singer and songwriter. Blending elements of Fuji music with contemporary Afropop and Amapiano, he rose to international prominence in the early 2020s, defining a new era of street-pop anthems. Known for his energetic performances and unique vocal delivery, Asake continues to top charts globally, representing the vibrant sound of Lagos on the world stage.
